Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about commissioning your heirloom bridal blouse with us.

We recommend commissioning your bridal blouse 4 to 8 weeks before your wedding date. Because each piece requires 40 to 120+ hours of focused hand-embroidery on wooden maggam frames, early booking guarantees your preferred trial date. We also accommodate emergency rush orders (within 10–14 days) depending on frame slot availability.

You can order completely remotely! More than 40% of our brides reside in Chennai, Bangalore, Coimbatore, Hyderabad, and overseas. We conduct structured WhatsApp video consultations where we inspect your saree, guide you through self-measurement or well-fitting sample blouse shipping, and share regular work-in-progress photos.

Yes, absolutely. Matching antique gold, copper zari, champagne gold, or silver zari to the specific weave of your wedding silk is our signature specialty. We source high-grade non-tarnishing metallic threads and dye silk threads to ensure flawless harmony with your saree’s pallu and border.

Computer/machine embroidery produces flat, stiff, and repetitive patterns with synthetic threads that often pucker delicate pure silk. Kisna’s authentic Aari hand-embroidery is worked stitch-by-stitch with an Aari needle on timber frames, producing three-dimensional depth, flexible drape, soft comfortable backing, and true heirloom longevity.

Bring it on! You can share any screenshot or sketch with our designer. We will adapt the concept to flatter your unique body measurements, sleeve length, and saree palette while elevating it with authentic zardozi craftsmanship.

Every blouse crafted at Kisna includes a hypoallergenic, ultra-soft pure cotton or mul-mul interior lining that completely encases the embroidery knots. The metallic work never touches your bare skin, ensuring you stay comfortable through hours of rituals, stage photography, and celebrations.

Our custom embroidery work starts from ₹3,000 for elegant minimal necklines, ₹5,000 to ₹10,000 for detailed bridal neck and sleeve work, and ₹12,000 to ₹25,000+ for dense, royal 3D zardozi and full-back portrait creations. We tailor the intricacy to your exact budget without compromising quality.

To lock your bridal date on our maggam frame schedule, we require a 50% advance upon final sketch approval. The remaining 50% balance is payable only after your trial fitting or video preview approval prior to final pickup/courier dispatch.
